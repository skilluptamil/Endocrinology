Trending Endocrine Health Topics

Quick clinical takeaways summarizing modern consensus in diabetes and hormonal medicine.

Glycemic Target
Time-in-Range (TIR) vs A1c

The ADA now recommends aiming for > 70% Time-in-Range (70-180 mg/dL). Two patients can have an identical 7.0% A1c, but one suffers severe highs and dangerous lows while the other remains steadily stable.

Thyroid Insight
Selenium & TPO Autoantibodies

Multiple double-blind randomized clinical trials demonstrate that selenomethionine supplementation (200 mcg/day) reduces circulating anti-TPO antibodies by 20-35% in Hashimoto’s thyroiditis.

Metabolic Fact
Visceral Adiposity & Liver Health

Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) affects up to 70% of people with Type 2 diabetes. Early insulin sensitization halts fibrotic progression before liver damage ensues.
