Privacy Policy & Health Data Protection

Section 1

Overview & Notice of Privacy Practices

EndoCare Endocrinology & Diabetes Care Clinic ("EndoCare", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of your Protected Health Information (PHI). This Notice of Privacy Practices describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information under federal HIPAA and applicable state laws.

We are required by law to maintain the privacy of protected health information, to provide individuals with notice of our legal duties and privacy practices with respect to protected health information, and to notify affected individuals following a breach of unsecured protected health information.

Section 2

Protected Health Information (PHI) & CGM Telemetry Data

In providing specialized endocrinology, diabetes management, hormone balancing, and metabolic therapies, we collect and process several categories of patient data:

Clinical & Demographic Data

Legal name, contact information, insurance policy details, clinical medical history, diagnostic ultrasound imaging, and lab pathology panels.

Continuous Sensor Telemetry

Automated sensor data streaming from authorized devices (Dexcom G6/G7, Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3, smart insulin pens, closed-loop pumps).

Sensor telemetry is synchronized exclusively via encrypted, authenticated API channels into our HIPAA-compliant physician monitoring portal. You retain the ability to revoke sensor telemetry permissions at any time through your EndoCare Patient Portal settings.

Section 3

How We Use and Disclose Clinical Data

Under federal law, your Protected Health Information may be used and disclosed without your specific written authorization for the following purposes:

  • Treatment: Providing, coordinating, or managing your endocrinology care, including interdisciplinary consultations between your attending endocrinologist, certified diabetes educators (CDCES), and clinical nutritionists.
  • Payment: Transmitting insurance claims, prior authorizations for GLP-1/GIP agonists, continuous glucose monitors, and verifying eligibility with your health insurance carrier.
  • Healthcare Operations: Quality assessment, peer review, electronic health record audits, and clinic operational analytics aimed at continually improving glycemic outcomes.
  • Telehealth Services: Transmitting video, audio, and vital metric tele-consultation data through our end-to-end encrypted virtual visit infrastructure.
Section 4

Cybersecurity, Encryption & Storage Standards

EndoCare maintains rigorous technical, administrative, and physical safeguards exceeding industry benchmarks to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of patient records:

AES-256 Encryption
At rest and in transit
SOC 2 Type II
Annual third-party audit
Multi-Factor Auth
Mandatory staff 2FA
Section 5

Your Rights Regarding Protected Health Information

As an EndoCare patient, you hold specific legal rights concerning your health records:

You have the right to inspect and obtain an electronic or paper copy of your medical and billing records. We provide direct export of your lab results and continuous glucose logs through the Patient Portal within 3 business days of your request.

You may request that we communicate with you by alternative means or at alternative locations (such as cell phone rather than home landline, or via encrypted secure messaging).

If you believe that your health record is incomplete or inaccurate, you may submit a formal request for an amendment in writing to our Compliance Officer.
Section 6

Diagnostic Laboratories & Business Associates

We coordinate with vetted clinical diagnostic partners (including LabCorp, Quest Diagnostics, and specialized molecular genetics laboratories for ThyroSeq testing). All third-party entities execute binding Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) legally binding them to identical HIPAA privacy safeguards.

Section 7

Privacy Officer & Reporting Grievances

If you have questions regarding this notice or believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with our Privacy Officer or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). You will not be retaliated against for filing a complaint.
