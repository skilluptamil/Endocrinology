Beyond Fingersticks: Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM)

Traditional fingerprick testing only captures single moments in time. Continuous glucose monitoring provides 288 readings every day, giving our physicians continuous visibility into nocturnal drops, post-meal spikes, and overall glycemic stability.

Predictive Hypoglycemia Alerts

Care Journey

Your 4-Step Pathway to Glycemic Freedom

How our clinical team guides you from unstable glucose swings to predictable, sustained metabolic equilibrium.

01
Metabolic Evaluation

We measure HbA1c, fasting insulin, microalbuminuria, lipids, and baseline cardiovascular markers to map your current baseline.

02
Sensor Attachment

Our clinical educator applies a gentle CGM sensor to your upper arm and connects it to your smartphone and patient portal.

03
Precision Care Plan

Your physician designs a tailored medical regimen combining optimal medications with carbohydrate-conscious meal planning.

04
Continuous Remote Care

Our clinical team reviews your bi-weekly glucose trends, adjusting doses remotely and answering questions before problems arise.
