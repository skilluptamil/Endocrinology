Physician Biography

Root-Cause Endocrinology Dedicated to Hormonal Restoration

"Rather than merely managing symptoms with oral contraceptives or isolated prescriptions, our mission is to understand the intricate neuro-endocrine and metabolic pathways that govern your hormones."

Dr. Elena Rostova completed her dual MD and PhD in Molecular Endocrinology at Stanford University School of Medicine, followed by internal medicine residency at Brigham & Women’s Hospital / Harvard Medical School. She then completed specialized endocrine fellowship training at the Mayo Clinic, zeroing in on ovarian steroidogenesis, hyperinsulinemia, and metabolic dysfunction.

Throughout her 14 years in academic and clinical practice, Dr. Rostova has built one of the nation's most respected outpatient programs for Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS). Her clinical protocol unites continuous glucose sensor telemetry (CGM), personalized micro-nutrient and inositol balance, insulin-sensitizing pharmacotherapies (including GLP-1/GIP agonists when clinically indicated), and targeted hormonal therapies.

Insulin Resistance Reversal

Early hyperinsulinemia detection before elevated fasting glucose appears. Real-time CGM sensor mapping, Kraft insulin curves, and metabolic restoration protocols.

  • Continuous glucose telemetry
  • Pre-diabetes remission pathways
Medical Weight Management

Comprehensive metabolic evaluation with dual GLP-1 and GIP receptor agonist therapy (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide) combined with muscle preservation strategies.

  • Metabolic baseline & DEXA scanning
  • Long-term taper & maintenance
Hormonal Fertility & Preconception

Optimizing ovulatory function, luteal phase progesterone balance, and endometrial receptivity for natural conception or assisted reproductive technology (ART/IVF).

  • Follicular phase tracking
  • Gestational diabetes risk reduction
Metabolic Liver & MASLD Care

Reversing hepatic steatosis (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease) through hepatic insulin sensitization, FIB-4 biomarker scoring, and nutrition.

  • Hepatic ultrasound surveillance
  • Cardiometabolic risk mitigation
Perimenopausal Metabolic Care

Navigating estrogen-progesterone decline, visceral adiposity redistribution, thyroid-adrenal interplay, and evidence-based bioidentical hormone therapy (BHRT).

  • Transdermal bioidentical hormones
