Patient Expectations

Your First Visit: What to Expect

We prioritize thorough listening and unhurried consultations so you leave with clarity, confidence, and a clear action plan.

01
Pre-Visit Review

We collect your historical records, previous glucose logs, and prior lab assays through our secure online portal before you arrive.

02
In-Depth Consultation

A 45-to-60 minute comprehensive session exploring your energy levels, sleep quality, dietary patterns, and physical symptoms.

03
Same-Day Testing

If necessary, ultrasound imaging or point-of-care HbA1c/lipid capillary blood sampling is completed on-site without extra travel.

04
Tailored Prescription

You receive a written clinical roadmap, sensor prescriptions if indicated, and access to our 24/7 patient portal messaging.
