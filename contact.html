Severe Endocrine Emergency Protocols

EndoCare Clinic is an outpatient subspecialty center. If you are experiencing symptoms of acute Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA), severe hypoglycemic unresponsiveness (< 54 mg/dL not responding to fast-acting glucose or glucagon), thyroid storm, or acute chest pain, please call 911 or proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room.

For non-life-threatening after-hours dosage queries, active patients may call our 24/7 on-call triage answering service at +1 (800) 555-ENDO.
Emergency: Call 911
