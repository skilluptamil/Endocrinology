Section 1

Clinical Relationship & Scope of Care

These Terms of Care govern the clinical services provided by EndoCare Endocrinology & Diabetes Care Clinic ("EndoCare") and its board-certified physicians, certified diabetes care and education specialists (CDCES), clinical pharmacists, and medical staff.

A physician-patient relationship is established exclusively after an initial clinical consultation has occurred, clinical intake documents are verified, and an attending physician has formally accepted your care. Merely visiting this website or requesting an appointment slot does not by itself establish a physician-patient relationship.

Section 2

Medical Emergency Disclaimer

Do Not Use This Website or Portal for Medical Emergencies

If you are experiencing severe hypoglycemia (blood glucose below 54 mg/dL accompanied by disorientation or inability to swallow), diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) symptoms, chest pain, difficulty breathing, or acute endocrine shock, IMMEDIATELY CALL 911 or go to the nearest emergency hospital.

Our website portal, secure message system, and remote sensor dashboards are monitored during standard business hours only and are not intended for real-time crisis management.

Section 3

Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) & Telehealth Agreement

Patients enrolled in our Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) program agree to the continuous streaming of sensor telemetry from authorized devices (Dexcom G7, Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3, smart pens):

  • Non-Continuous Surveillance: Sensor telemetry is analyzed during scheduled clinical review windows. It is not an active emergency alarm dispatch service.
  • Patient Verification: Patients must verify sensor accuracy with blood glucose meter fingerstick testing if sensor symptoms do not match readings or during rapid glycemic rate changes.
  • Telehealth Consent: By participating in video visits, you certify that you are physically located in a state where your attending physician is licensed to practice medicine.
Section 4

Appointment Scheduling & 24-Hour Cancellation Policy

Because endocrine and metabolic evaluations involve dedicated 30 to 60-minute physician time blocks, we enforce a strict 24-hour cancellation protocol:

24-Hour Advance Notice

Cancellations or reschedules must be made at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled in-clinic or telehealth appointment.

Late Cancellation / No-Show Fee

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice or missed appointments incur a $50 no-show fee (not covered by insurance).

Section 5

Prescription Refill Policies & Prior Authorizations

To ensure patient safety, medication refills for insulin, thyroid replacement, GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists (e.g. Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy), and hormone therapies require:

  • Documented routine follow-up consultations at intervals specified by your endocrinologist (typically every 3 to 6 months).
  • Up-to-date diagnostic lab surveillance (such as HbA1c, renal eGFR/creatinine panels, thyroid TSH/Free T4, and liver enzymes).
  • A minimum of 72 business hours notice for prescription refill processing and insurance prior authorization renewal requests.
Section 6

Insurance Billing, Copays & Financial Policy

EndoCare contracts with most major commercial insurance carriers and Medicare. However:

Patient Financial Responsibility: You are ultimately responsible for payment of all services rendered, including deductibles, co-payments, and co-insurance.

Copay Collection: In accordance with insurance contractual agreements, all applicable copayments are due at the time of check-in before service delivery.

Self-Pay & Direct Care: Transparent self-pay package pricing is published on our Pricing Page for uninsured patients or non-covered holistic protocols.

Section 7

Code of Conduct & Contact

We are committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and therapeutic clinical environment. Verbal abuse, harassment, or threats toward medical staff will result in immediate termination of the clinical relationship and referral to emergency services.
